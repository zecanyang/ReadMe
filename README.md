# Hi, I'm Zecan Yang 👋

**Computer Researcher @ Zhengzhou University**

I develop **Robust, Interpretable, and Efficient learning methods for Complex High-dimensional Data Analysis**.

I received my Ph.D. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology, supervised by Prof. Laurence T. Yang. My research connects Bayesian learning, tensor methods, and adaptive learning, with applications in computer vision, spatio-temporal data analysis, and collaborative intelligence.

[Website](https://zecanyang.github.io/) · [Publications](https://zecanyang.github.io/publications) · [Experience](https://zecanyang.github.io/experience)

## Research Interests

- **Probabilistic & Bayesian Learning** — uncertainty quantification, scalable inference.
- **Tensor Methods & Structured Representations** — tensor decomposition, completion, and low-rank modeling of multiway data.
- **Robust & Efficient Learning** — reliable learning from incomplete and noisy data, with efficient models for practical deployment.
- **Federated Learning & Privacy-Preserving Learning** — personalized learning and privacy-aware collaboration across distributed data sources.
